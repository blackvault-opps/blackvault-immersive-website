HOW TO PLAY — SIGNAL BREAK™

A signal indicator sweeps across the scanner bar. Press BREAK SIGNAL when the signal is inside the purple synchronization zone.

PERFECT — center of zone → +75 XP
STRONG — middle zone → +40 XP
WEAK — outer zone → +15 XP
MISS — outside zone → +0 XP

Consecutive hits build your streak multiplier (up to x3). Random anomaly events change signal speed or zone width.

Mission: Break 15 signals to unlock the "SIGNAL ANALYST" achievement.
